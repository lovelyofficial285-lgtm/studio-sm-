About

Welcome to StudioSM.

StudioSM is a modern hair salon where style meets comfort. We are all about creating looks that fit your personality — whether that is a bold new colour, a fresh cut or a sleek, effortless finish.

Our team of skilled stylists takes the time to understand your vision and bring it to life using quality products and techniques. At StudioSM, it is more than just a haircut — it is your time to relax, refresh and walk out feeling confident.
